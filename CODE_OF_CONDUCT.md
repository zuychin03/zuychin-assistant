# Code of conduct

Zuychin Assistant is a small project, and we want everyone who takes part to feel welcome. This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else you represent the project.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, and assume good faith.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Respect different levels of experience, backgrounds and points of view.
- Accept a decision gracefully, even when it isn't the one you wanted.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory or sexual language and imagery.
- Personal attacks, trolling or deliberately derailing a conversation.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Posting other people's conversations, notes or vault pages without their consent.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour breaks this code, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment, issue or pull request, or contact the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments and contributions, and may block anyone who doesn't follow this code.

This code is based on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
